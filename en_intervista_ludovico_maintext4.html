[Interviewer]: One last question, Maestro. If you had to leave a memory of Via del Porto for those who walk here four hundred years from now, what would you say to them?



[Ludovico Carracci]: I would tell them not to just look at the stone or the facades of churches. I would tell them to close their eyes and listen. Listen to the flowing of the underground water, the beating of the clothes, the determined step of those who work. And I would tell them that great art is not born from abstract formulas, but from the ability to look at everyday reality with profound compassion and love. As long as there is a mirror of water reflecting the sky between the houses of the Port, our painting will live on.
