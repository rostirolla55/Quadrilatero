Ten years later (1865) the residence of the Rector of the Church was destroyed and the current apse, large and elegant, was built and the facade was completed (1866). Furthermore, the other altars were embellished and two new chapels were built, one of which, the one dedicated to St. Joseph, in which there is a beautiful statue of the Saint, by Angelo Piò, was reserved for the Marquis Giuseppe Davia, who with truly singular munificence, supported all the costs of the works, begun by the priest Rectors of the Church, but which could not be completed due to lack of means.

A new residence was also built for the Rector of the Church, which is the current one. All the aforementioned works, which gave the Church its complete structure, were completed in 1874.
