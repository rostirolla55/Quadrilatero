The Madonna Fata is a terracotta statue preserved under the portico of Via Saragozza (at the height of the Arch of the Annunziata, near the intersection with Via Bellombra), along the historic route that leads up to the Sanctuary of the Madonna di San Luca.

Sculpted in 1749 by the Bolognese sculptor Andrea Ferreri , the statue depicts the Virgin with the Child in her arms. Its distinctive feature—hence the irreverent yet affectionate nickname given to it by the Bolognese—lies in its decidedly florid and matronly forms, typical of the Baroque style and prosperity of Emilia.
