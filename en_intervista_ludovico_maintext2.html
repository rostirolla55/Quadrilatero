[Ludovico Carracci]: (Smiles, dusting off his pigment-covered hands) And where should I be, pray? Among the cold marbles of the Senate? No, my dear. Here the blood of Bologna truly flows! Do you hear that noise? It's the water from the canals that drives the mills and feeds the dye works . The Port is the beating heart of the city: there are the goods arriving from the Po, the boatmen who curse, the friars of San Carlo who pray, and the washerwomen who know everything about everyone. This is not just water: it is life, it is movement, it is light that changes every hour.
