The clock mounted at the top of the Church of San Carlo, a timekeeping landmark for the entire district and the two Seminaries, was built in 1865 by Antonio Franchini and strikes hours and quarters in the Italian style; the quarter-hour bell bears Brighenti's signature and the date 1831. Commemorative booklet for the Eucharistic Decennial of S. Maria Maggiore / Church of S. Carlo, Bologna, 1939; Archives of the Church of Ss. Carlo ed Ambrogio, Bologna (19th-century expense ledgers).
The San Carlo complex houses Bolognese paintings including the canvas depicting "Saint Aloysius Gonzaga in Ecstasy" attributed to Filippo Pedrini and "The Assumption of the Virgin" by Marcantonio Franceschini. Marcello Oretti, "Notizie de' professori del disegno" (Archiginnasio Municipal Library, Bologna); General Catalogue of Cultural Heritage (Italian Ministry of Culture - Bologna Superintendency).
The Oratory adjoining the Church of San Carlo, used as a meeting hall and "school" for the confraternity members, preserved its original decorative scheme intact and is listed as a national monument. Ministry of Public Instruction / Superintendency of Monuments of Bologna, List of Monumental Buildings (Province of Bologna); Guido Zucchini, "Edifici di Bologna", Rome, 1931.
The paintings and decorations of the Oratory were executed between 1650 and 1660: according to Carlo Cesare Malvasia and Orlandi, confraternity member Giacomo Friani painted the chiaroscuro and ornament with Domenico Martelli (cited by Bianconi), while Guido Reni's pupil Giovanni Battista Bolognini the Elder (1611-1688) painted the figures. Carlo Cesare Malvasia, "Le pitture di Bologna", Bologna, 1686; Pellegrino Antonio Orlandi, "Abecedario pittorico", Bologna, 1704; Girolamo Bianconi, "Guida del forestiere per la città di Bologna", 1820.
Corrado Ricci attributed the overall design and artistic direction of the Oratory's decoration to Angelo Michele Colonna (1600-1687), leading master of Bolognese quadratura painting, who coordinated the work of Friani, Martelli, and Bolognini. Corrado Ricci, "Guida di Bologna", Zanichelli, Bologna (late 19th - early 20th-century editions); Ebria Feinblatt, studies on Angelo Michele Colonna and 17th-century Bolognese quadratura.
Between late August and November 1936, Rector Monsignor Alfonso Brini promoted the complete restoration of the Oratory and its vault frescoes (centered on the Glory of Saint Charles), at a cost of about 40,000 lire with technical oversight by the Superintendency of Medieval and Modern Art, ahead of the First Eucharistic Decennial. Commemorative booklet for the Eucharistic Decennial of the Church of S. Carlo, 1939; Historical Archives of the Bologna Superintendency, Restoration files 1936-1937 (Oratory of San Carlo).
The lower walls of the Oratory are lined with an authentic eighteenth-century walnut wainscoting; during the works promoted by Monsignor Alfonso Brini, two canvases depicting Saint Vincent Ferrer and Saint Francis of Paola—attributed by critics to the Gandolfi workshop—were discovered in a storeroom near the organ stairs and restored. Commemorative booklet for the Eucharistic Decennial of the Church of S. Carlo, 1939; Donatella Biagi Maino, "Ubaldo Gandolfi" / "Gaetano Gandolfi", Turin; General Catalogue of Cultural Heritage (MiC).
