[Narrator] Setting: Bologna, early seventeenth century. We are a stone's throw from the church of San Carlo and Via del Porto. The air smells of hanging laundry, lye soap, and oil pigments. From the open windows overlooking the pugliole , we can hear the rhythmic thump of clothes floating on the Grada canal and the lively chatter of washerwomen. Ludovico is sitting on a wooden stool, grinding red ochre on a stone slab.



[Interviewer]: Maestro Ludovico, thank you for welcoming us. It's rare to find a painter of your renown in such a hard-working and humble neighborhood, far from the gilded buildings of the city center. Why here, between the Navile and Via del Porto?
