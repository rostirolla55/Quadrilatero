The Church of San Carlo in Bologna



(Historical notes)

Various names from the 15th century onwards



The Church of Saints Charles and Ambrose, clean and tidy, with simple lines and a modest air, yet not without grace, is one of those buildings battered by the years and the changing opinions of men, almost forgotten and revived, unsafe and rebuilt, and one of those monuments that, alongside the conventional and cold air of modernization, unexpectedly acquires the appearance and importance of a work of art.



Once on the corner of Via dei Morelli and Via del Maglio, the Seminary now surrounds it on all sides. It faces the straight, low vista of Via S. Carlo, or Via Nuova , as our elders still insist on calling it, and truly dominates the place with its slender, elegant, smiling façade.
