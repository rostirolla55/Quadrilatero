MA FRANCESCHINI — The Assumption

If, by bad luck, the clock stops, the residents of the neighborhood even attack the sacristan. And rightly so, since the entire neighborhood is regulated by this clock, which, fortunately, works perfectly. It is the work of Antonio Franchini, who built it in 1865. It has a distinctive two-hand dial surmounted by a simple and elegant iron frame on which the bells are hung; it strikes the hours and quarters in the Italian style. The quarter bell is by Brighenti and dates back to 1831.

But let's go back to the Church.

After continuous changes, which have never ceased, throughout the centuries: " its permutations have no respite " (Dante), the Church of San Carlo has finally reached a definitive form, which it still retains today, full of grandeur, elegance and art.

To you, O Lord, I will approach as a beggar, because you, though rich, became poor for our sake; I will be enriched by your poverty. I will approach as a weak man, because you yourself have declared to us that not the healthy but the sick need a physician. I will approach as a lame man and I will pray: O Lord, direct my steps in your ways. I will approach as a blind man and I will beseech you; Enlighten my eyes, O Lord, that I may never sleep in death.

(St. Augustine)
