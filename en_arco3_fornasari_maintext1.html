Arch 3 Memorial Tablet of Abbot Ippolito Fornasari

The system of porticos that climbs to the Colle della Guardia was built thanks to an extraordinary collective participation: noble families, guilds of arts and crafts, but also wealthy individual citizens or religious figures financed the construction of one or more arches, leaving a tablet in memory of their contribution. Count Gioseffo Carpegna financed the construction of Arch 3 of the Portico di San Luca.
